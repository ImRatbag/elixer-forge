    Deck rules

    8 cards. Three special slots, always filled when you own enough: an Evo slot, a Hero slot (Hero or Champion) and a Wild slot. At most 2 Evos or 2 Hero/Champions, 1 Champion per deck, and each card in one form only. In 2v2 the two decks may share up to 2 cards.
